== Negative conformance fixtures

[[annex-negative-intro]]
=== Purpose

This annex lists the negative test cases N1 to N6 of the European DPP
corpus defect register (<<unidpp-audit>>, Section F: conformance-run
findings against <<en18223>> Annex A, machine-verified 2026-09-07).
They are reproduced here as conformance test material: every fixture
is an instance of a class of defect that conforming implementations
(Clauses 12.2 and 12.5) shall reject or flag. The fixtures also serve
as evidence for the framework's counter-principles (5.2): tool-checked
content and registry-validated semantics over manually maintained
prose examples.

[[annex-negative-fixtures]]
=== Fixtures

[[tbl-negative-fixtures]]
.Table A.1 — Negative test cases
[cols="8,26,66",options="header"]
|===
| # | Location | Defect and required behaviour

| N1 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 2 | Not well-formed JSON: misnested and unclosed braces; literal ellipsis placeholders in a normative annex. A conforming parser shall reject the document as not well-formed.
| N2 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 3 | A data-point reference `https:/dictionary1/...` omits the authority separator. A conforming resolver shall reject the reference as malformed and shall not silently repair it.
| N3 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 4 | A duplicate element identifier violates the document's own uniqueness requirement. A conforming validator shall reject the duplicate.
| N4 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 4 | Floating-point values serialized as strings contradict the document's own numeric type and compressed numeric form. A conforming validator shall reject the type violation.
| N5 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 4 versus Example 1 | The same class serialized with different member keys in adjacent normative examples. A conforming validator shall treat the two spellings as the same class normalized per 7.1 e, or reject the non-canonical form; drift between normative examples is itself a defect class.
| N6 | EN 18223 Annex A, Example 5 | A trailing comma renders the JSON invalid in a normative annex. A conforming parser shall reject the document as not well-formed.
|===

[[annex-negative-mapping]]
=== Defect classes

Each fixture instantiates one of the framework's seam failures
(5.10): N1 and N6 are serialization defects (core renders); N2 is a
seam S4 failure (profile to registry resolution); N3 is an
identity-uniqueness failure; N4 and N5 are normalization failures that
the framework's casing and separator normalization (7.1 e) and its
single canonical body rule (8.1) preclude at construction. The
underlying corpus-level lesson is documented in <<unidpp-audit>>: all
six normative annex examples of <<en18223>> fail machine validation,
while the profile adaptations (casing normalization, field-name
mapping) that reconcile the EU profile family with the neutral core
are exactly the profile mechanism of Clause 6 at work.

[[annex-instance-negatives]]
=== The instance-intake negative fixtures

The negative fixtures of the instance intake are conformance
material in the discipline of this annex. A conforming
implementation shall refuse each of the following registrations, and
shall state the refusal as this table describes.

[[tbl-instance-negatives]]
.Table A.2 — Instance-intake negative fixtures
[cols="10,22,68",options="header"]
|===
| # | Fixture | Defect and required behaviour

| IC1 | missing-required | The instance omits a fact that the descriptor declares required. The registration shall refuse and shall name the fact path and the descriptor's requirement.
| IC2 | wrong-kind | The instance carries a fact at a declared path as a value of a kind other than the kind the descriptor assigns. The registration shall refuse and shall name the fact path and both kinds.
| IC3 | out-of-vocabulary | The instance carries a value outside the vocabulary the descriptor declares for a string feature. The registration shall refuse and shall name the member and the declared vocabulary.
| IC4 | unresolved-unit | The descriptor references a unit that no registered unit item names at the instant of registration. The registration shall refuse and shall name the unit reference.
| IC5 | unknown-class | The instance names, as `instance_of`, an identifier that no registered class descriptor carries, or that another class carries. The registration shall refuse and shall name the class identifier.
|===

[[annex-rule-negatives]]
=== The rule-expression negative fixtures

The negative fixtures of the rule-expression reading are conformance
material in the discipline of this annex. A conforming
implementation shall refuse each of the following registrations, and
shall state the refusal as this table describes.

[[tbl-rule-negatives]]
.Table A.3 — Rule-expression negative fixtures
[cols="10,22,68",options="header"]
|===
| # | Fixture | Defect and required behaviour

| RC1 | unparseable-expression | A rule declaration whose expression the grammar does not accept. The descriptor registration shall refuse and shall name the parse failure and its position.
| RC2 | dangling-reference | A rule reference naming no rule the descriptor declares. The descriptor registration shall refuse and shall name the reference.
| RC3 | undeclared-path | A rule expression naming a fact path the descriptor does not declare. The descriptor registration shall refuse and shall name the path.
| RC4 | kind-conflict | A rule expression pitting a literal against a feature of another kind, or ordering a non-decimal feature. The descriptor registration shall refuse and shall name the path and both kinds.
| RC5 | unsatisfied-rule | An instance for which a referenced rule evaluates false. The instance registration shall refuse and shall name the rule's identifier and its expression.
|===
