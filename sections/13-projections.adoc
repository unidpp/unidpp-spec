[[cl-projections]]
== Projections

[[cl-projections-calculus]]
=== The projection calculus

Every published projection SHALL carry its five-axis descriptor,
machine-readable: temporal (point-in-time · continuous), content
class (static · dynamic · mixed), derivation (direct · derived),
exchange encoding (native · translated), granularity (type ·
instance · composite · genealogy). The axes are independent; each
named projection type lives on exactly one axis; concrete
projections are tuples.

Two requirements follow from the calculus:

* *Replayability*: event sourcing implies that every point-in-time
  projection is recomputable from the log alone; as-of is a query,
  not archaeology. Implementations SHALL NOT require auxiliary
  state to reconstruct a point-in-time projection.
* *Honest degradation*: a continuous projection without a current
  freshness verdict SHALL degrade explicitly (stating the missing
  verdict), never pass silently.

[[cl-projections-freeze]]
=== Freeze

Freeze turns a continuous projection into a point-in-time one: the
operation SHALL refuse a descriptor already declared
point-in-time-less (freezing is the point-in-time act) and SHALL
attach a verification bundle. The point-in-time form, once notarized
under trusted time (Clause 12), is immutable.

[[cl-frozen-view]]
=== Frozen views

A **frozen view** is the self-describing publication object — the
minimal interoperability unit between schemes (the F1 conformance
class: publishes verifiable frozen views). It SHALL carry:

a) the rendered payload;
b) the five-axis descriptor (temporal: point-in-time — enforced);
c) the lens metadata: the profile reference, the transform chain
   with per-step references and versions (registered, deterministic,
   cited), and the named input channels;
d) the committed inputs: the open-class bytes the lens consumes,
   each pinned by the segment state commitment in the carried spine;
e) the verification bundle (the dossier: signed policies, signed
   spine with inclusion proofs, attestations, the journaled S13
   exchange, and the log receipt when served);
f) reader instructions: the verification checks, in order.

Verification of a frozen view SHALL succeed using the view alone and
the verifier's own anchors: the bundle verifies (12.7), every input
matches its spine commitment, and every input is a declared lens
channel — unexplained inputs are refused. **Re-execution**: the
verifier re-executes the lens over the committed inputs and SHALL
obtain the issuer-side payload byte-identically; a mismatch is a
failure, not a warning. The canonical encoding of the frozen view
follows Annex B; its golden vectors are conformance material.

A scheme that publishes verifiable frozen views — verifiable by any
third party with its own anchors and no connection to the publisher
— is F1 regardless of what software it runs.
