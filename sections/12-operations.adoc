[[cl-operations]]
== Operations and durability

The framework's guarantees survive contact with operations or they do
not exist. This clause states the operational invariants as normative
requirements; each is demonstrated by the reference operator tooling
(`unidpp-ops` and the pilot deployment, see the bibliography's
implementation references).

[[cl-ops-data]]
=== The deployment as data

A deployment comprises its configuration (the operator manifest), its
services' journals, and its seed assets. Services SHALL reconstruct
their state by journal replay on start; no service SHALL hold
authoritative state that is not journaled. Configuration SHALL be
externalized data — a deployment's identity, services, and policies
SHALL be expressible without code changes (the whitelabel and
sovereign profiles are configuration, not forks).

[[cl-ops-backup]]
=== Backup integrity

A backup of a deployment SHALL carry, for every member it contains,
an integrity value (SHA-256) in an accompanying manifest, together
with the UTC moment of the snapshot and a consistency point: the
transparency log's signed tree head at snapshot time, delimiting what
a restore replays up to.

[[cl-ops-restore]]
=== Restore safety

A restore SHALL verify every member's integrity value before
unpacking any file. A restore SHALL refuse to overwrite an existing
deployment unless explicitly instructed; when instructed, it SHALL
preserve the replaced state (set aside, never destroyed). Member
paths belonging to the backed-up deployment's own tenant SHALL be
rewritten into the restored deployment; members belonging to other
tenants SHALL be restored as archive material under their original
identities (a restore SHALL NOT collapse distinct tenants onto shared
paths).

Recovery to the deployment's original shape (disaster recovery)
SHALL follow the same rules: verified before unpacking, the replaced
state set aside and never destroyed, and every member restored at
its original path — no rewriting of any kind.

[[cl-ops-append-only]]
=== Append-only continuity

Service journals are append-only across the service lifecycle: a
service upgrade or restart SHALL NOT rewrite, reorder, or truncate
any journal. The journals captured by a pre-upgrade backup SHALL be
byte-prefixes of the corresponding post-upgrade journals. An operator
SHALL be able to demonstrate this property by rehearsal: record the
domain state, rebuild and restart the services under the new
binaries, and show the readings equal and the prefix property holds.

[[cl-ops-drill]]
=== Proven restore

A backup that has never been restored is a claim, not a capability.
An operator deployment SHALL demonstrate its restore path by
rehearsal — backup, restore into an isolated rehearsal target owned
by the rehearsal itself, verify checksums, confirm restored members
byte-identical, and validate the restored configuration — and SHALL
record the rehearsal's outcome as an artifact. The rehearsal SHALL
NOT touch production state.

[[cl-ops-bundle]]
=== Transfer artifacts

Where a deployment is transferred to another host (on-premise or
sovereign operation), the transfer artifact SHALL carry per-member
integrity metadata verifiable independently of its producer, and
SHALL NOT embed secret material: secrets travel as environment
references, resolved at the receiving deployment.
